# Spotlight Iasi Design Guide

This repository follows the Toastmasters International brand manual in `docs/brand-manual.pdf`.
Use this file as the working design reference for the website.

## Brand Summary

Spotlight Iasi should feel:

- confident, warm, and professional
- clear and easy to scan
- supportive, inclusive, and member focused
- consistent with Toastmasters brand rules

The site is a club presentation and landing experience, not a marketing-heavy or decorative brochure site.
Favor structure, clarity, and trust over visual noise.

## Brand Values

The Toastmasters core values are:

- Respect
- Integrity
- Service
- Excellence

These should show up in copy, interaction design, and visual hierarchy.
Keep the tone positive, clear, respectful, and concise.

## Color Palette

Use the official Toastmasters palette as the base system:

- Loyal Blue: `#004165`
- True Maroon: `#772432`
- Cool Gray: `#A9B2B1`
- Happy Yellow: `#F2DF74`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Black: `#000000`

Guidelines:

- Loyal Blue and True Maroon are the primary brand colors for headers, emphasis, and large surfaces.
- Cool Gray is for neutral backgrounds and subtle structure.
- Happy Yellow is an accent only. Use it sparingly for highlights or emphasis.
- Maintain strong contrast between text and background.
- Do not create new brand hues or introduce unrelated gradients.

Approved gradient direction for this site:

- Loyal Blue to a slightly brighter blue for the header background
- subtle, restrained gradients only

## Typography

Toastmasters uses:

- Gotham for headlines and subheads
- Myriad Pro for body copy

Free alternates:

- Montserrat for Gotham
- Source Sans 3 for Myriad Pro

Rules:

- Use bold, geometric, clear headline styling.
- Keep body text readable at small sizes.
- Do not use decorative effects such as drop shadows or stylized word art.
- Avoid exaggerated letter spacing or type distortions.

Recommended usage in this repo:

- `font-heading`: Montserrat
- `font-sans`: Source Sans 3

## Logo and Identity

Important rules from the manual:

- Do not create custom Toastmasters logos, themes, or taglines.
- Use the official Toastmasters logo and wordmark as provided.
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to the logo height.
- Respect minimum size rules.
- Do not alter the logo colors, proportions, or shape.

For web use:

- use the official logo assets as provided
- prefer a simple icon or wordmark treatment over custom artwork
- keep logo placement clean and uncluttered

## Photography

Photography should look:

- engaged
- empowered
- supported
- natural and credible

Use images of:

- meetings
- speeches
- networking
- training
- club members and officers

Avoid imagery that is unrelated to the Toastmasters environment, such as:

- generic landscapes
- food
- architecture
- stock-like filler visuals
- decorative illustrations as primary imagery

When using photography:

- keep strong contrast
- avoid obscuring faces
- do not overcrowd the image with text
- use Toastmasters-approved or club-owned photos only

## Layout Principles

The site should feel:

- structured
- calm
- functional
- easy to scan

Use:

- clear section hierarchy
- short headlines
- concise supporting copy
- obvious calls to action
- consistent spacing

Avoid:

- oversized marketing hero pages
- card-heavy decorative layouts
- cluttered multi-color compositions
- unnecessary motion

Recommended page structure:

- a strong hero section with clear club identity
- supporting information blocks with simple hierarchy
- officer and team sections with portrait images
- practical contact and navigation access

## Components

Use common UI patterns consistently:

- buttons for primary actions
- separators for section breaks
- tabs or segmented controls when comparing views
- menus for option sets
- icons inside buttons where helpful
- cards only for repeated items or genuinely framed content

Card guidance:

- keep cards restrained and functional
- prefer 8px radius or less unless a component already defines otherwise
- avoid nested cards
- avoid floating card stacks for page sections

## Accessibility

Always keep:

- text readable against its background
- focus states visible
- tap targets large enough on mobile
- semantic headings in correct order
- alt text on meaningful images

Do not rely on color alone to communicate meaning.

## Website Rules

The website guidelines in the manual require a disclaimer at the bottom of the site:

> The information on this website is for the sole use of Toastmasters' members, for Toastmasters business only. It is not to be used for solicitation and distribution of non-Toastmasters material or information.

Keep that disclaimer present in the footer.

Additional web guidance:

- use consistent brand colors and typography across all pages
- keep the club identity clear at the top of the site
- avoid re-hosting protected Toastmasters material unless allowed
- use approved assets and photos

## Site-Specific Direction

For this repository:

- keep the site light-only
- preserve the Toastmasters header treatment
- keep the footer structured and informational
- keep landing pages presentation-oriented but not promotional
- use the official brand palette and typography throughout
- make officer and club imagery feel editorial, clean, and sincere

## Practical Guardrails

Before shipping UI changes, check:

- does it still feel like Toastmasters?
- does the color usage stay within brand rules?
- is the text clear and readable?
- are photos relevant and respectful?
- are sections easy to scan?
- does the page keep the disclaimer visible in the footer?

If a design choice is flashy but not useful, simplify it.
If a section can be clearer, make it clearer.
